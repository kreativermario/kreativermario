## 🐧 About

Homelab tinkerer. I like penguins, self-hosting things, and used enterprise gear.
Most of my free time goes into the rack below: networking, virtualisation, storage
and whatever Kubernetes distro I'm currently fighting with.

---

## 🔬 Homelab

Small lab for personal projects and learning. Always looking out for used enterprise
equipment (hmu if you're selling anything!) to build the full rack: switches, routers,
firewalls, servers and UPS.

### 💻 Hardware

| Node | Specs | OS |
|---|---|---|
| Hypervisor | i7-14700 · 64GB DDR5-6000 · 1TB NVMe · 30TB HDD + parity | [Proxmox VE](https://www.proxmox.com/) |
| Edge | Raspberry Pi 4B 4GB | Raspberry Pi OS |

### 📦 What runs on it

**Virtualisation & orchestration:** [Proxmox](https://www.proxmox.com/), [Docker](https://www.docker.com/), Dockhand, [OKD](https://www.okd.io/), [RKE2 / Rancher](https://docs.rke2.io/)

**Network & DNS:** [MikroTik](https://mikrotik.com/), [OPNsense](https://opnsense.org/), [Technitium](https://technitium.com/dns/), BIND, [WireGuard](https://www.wireguard.com/)

**Ingress:** [HAProxy](https://www.haproxy.org/), [Traefik](https://traefik.io/)

**Storage:** [OpenMediaVault](https://www.openmediavault.org/) NAS with [SnapRAID](https://www.snapraid.it/) + [MergerFS](https://github.com/trapexit/mergerfs)

**Media & apps:** [Plex](https://www.plex.tv/), [SkyWizz](https://github.com/kreativermario/SkyWizz)
